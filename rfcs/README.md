says otherwise. The companion index of decisions owned elsewhere is

## Conventions

- **Status** is one of: Alpha (working, open), Accepted, Superseded, Rejected.
- An RFC that reaches Accepted may move into `docs/` and keep its number.
- Superseded RFCs keep their text. Replacing a number loses the reasoning.
- Date is when the RFC was opened, not last edited.
- Deliberately deferred questions stay in the RFC as named deferrals. A silent
  omission reads as a decision nobody made.
